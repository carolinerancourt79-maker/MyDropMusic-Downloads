# Privacy Policy — MyDropMusic

[Français](PRIVACY.md)

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This policy covers MyDropMusic for iPhone and its MyDropMusic Sync companion for Windows in their current versions.

## Developer and contact

MyDropMusic is developed by Caroline Rancourt. For privacy questions or support, contact [mydropmusic.support@gmail.com](mailto:mydropmusic.support@gmail.com).

## Music and data on your devices

MyDropMusic plays the music files you import. Songs, their organization, playlists, favorites, settings and synchronization information are stored on your devices. The Windows companion accesses the music folder you select to list available files and transfer them to your iPhone.

The app stores the PC address, pairing code and certificate fingerprint so it can connect again. This information is not sent to a server operated by the developer.

MyDropMusic has no user accounts, advertising, advertising tracking or integrated audience analytics tools. We do not automatically receive your music library or listening history, and we do not sell your data.

## Wi-Fi transfers and permissions

Synchronization between the iPhone and Windows companion takes place over your local network. Smart Sync transfers use HTTPS and verify the PC certificate fingerprint obtained during pairing.

Local network access allows the app to find your PC and transfer music. Camera access is used to scan the PC pairing QR code. The scanner does not take photos or record video. You can manage these permissions in iPhone Settings.

The companion may display local technical messages, including transfer requests and related file paths. These are not automatically sent to the developer. Avoid sharing your pairing code or QR code publicly.

## External links and support

The companion download button opens a GitHub page in your browser. GitHub, your email provider and Apple process information associated with their services under their own policies. This policy describes MyDropMusic, rather than those services.

If you contact us by email, we receive your address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use this information to reply and resolve your request. Do not send music files or personal information that is unnecessary for support. Correspondence is retained for the time needed to handle and follow up on your request; you can request deletion using the email address above.

## Deletion and control

You can delete songs and playlists using the app's controls. Deleting a song on the PC should not be treated as automatically deleting its copy on the iPhone.

Deleting the iPhone app removes its local data; the iOS offloading feature keeps documents and data. Any backup copies are managed separately through your backup services. On Windows, uninstalling the companion may leave settings and its certificate in the user's data folder; contact us if you want help removing them. Your original music files on the PC remain under your control.

## Updates

We will update this policy if MyDropMusic's functionality or data handling practices change. The date at the top indicates the latest update.
